One file, every open/deposit/close date for your current ladder, with 3-day-early reminders built in. Import it into Google Calendar (Settings → Import & export → Import), Apple Calendar (just double-click it) or Outlook — no terminal, no Python.

Not financial advice. Bonuses are taxable income, usually reported on a 1099-INT. Opening many accounts can trigger ChexSystems denials. Terms change without notice — open the source link on every offer and confirm the requirements with the bank before you act.

The plan is arithmetic over what each bank's page said on the date shown, not a prediction. The biggest unknown it cannot see is whether your employer's payment will actually code as a qualifying direct deposit at a given bank.
